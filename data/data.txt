2.1 SALES.CSV - Transaction-level Sales Data

3. DATA RELATIONSHIPS
================================================================================

PRIMARY RELATIONSHIPS:
  sales.VendorNo → purchases.VendorNumber → vendor_invoice.VendorNumber
  sales.Brand → purchase_prices.Brand
  sales.InventoryId → begin_inventory.InventoryId → end_inventory.InventoryId

COMPOSITE KEY PATTERN:
  InventoryId format: {Store}_{City}_{Brand}
  Example: 1_HARDERSFIELD_1004

INVENTORY EQUATION (Reconciled):
  EndingUnits = BeginningUnits + PurchaseUnits - SalesUnits
  Validation: Reconciles exactly at portfolio level


================================================================================
4. KEY BUSINESS DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

ESTIMATED COGS:
  Sales Quantity × Weighted Purchase Cost (same vendor-brand)

WEIGHTED GROSS MARGIN:
  (Total Sales - Total Estimated COGS) / Total Sales × 100%

SELL-THROUGH RATE:
  Sales Units / (Beginning Inventory + Purchased Units)

INVENTORY TURNOVER:
  Sales Units / Average(Beginning Units, Ending Units)

COST MATCHING:
  99.90% of sales have matched purchase costs from same vendor-brand
  Profitability excludes the unmatched 0.10%


================================================================================
5. DATA QUALITY NOTES
================================================================================

VENDOR NAME FORMATTING:
  - Vendor names are right-padded with spaces to fixed width
  - Use TRIM() when querying vendor names for clean results

NULL HANDLING:
  - Use NULLIF(denominator, 0) to prevent division by zero
  - COALESCE for left-joined purchase data that may not exist

TIME PERIODS:
  - Sales: Daily granularity throughout 2024
  - Inventory: Two snapshots (start: 2024-01-01, end: 2024-12-31)
  - Purchases: Transaction-level with multiple date fields

PRICE VARIATIONS:
  - Sales prices can vary by date (promotions, markdowns)
  - Purchase prices are aggregated using weighted average by vendor-brand
